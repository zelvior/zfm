# Pakistan checklist

**NEEDS REVIEW.** This is a practical starting point, not legal advice. Verify current law, amendments, commencement and fees with qualified local counsel and official authorities; Pakistan Code warns some content may be under review.

1. Establish the actual individual or entity, domicile, capacity, authority, contribution rights and any applicable employment agreements. If a minor or otherwise lacking legal capacity, seek an authorized adult and professional advice.
2. Copyright may arise without registration; [IPO Pakistan](https://www.ipo.gov.pk/copyright_intro) describes registration as voluntary and a certificate as prima facie evidence. Computer programs are addressed by the [Copyright Ordinance](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administratorc8dc84cf91fcedf0a4a00fc2710945a1.pdf). IPO's [procedure](https://ipo.gov.pk/system/files/Procedure%20for%20Registration%20%28Copyright%29_0.pdf) lists Form II, copies and originality affidavit and NOC/assignment where applicable. Verify the current form, fees, deposit and applicant eligibility; do not swear originality without a verified rights chain.
3. Trademark clearance and filing: [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md); consult the [Trade Marks Ordinance](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administratora4ef8d40e3d97faef49343d2242e0c3a.pdf).
4. Assess whether incorporation, tax registration or other filings apply to actual activities; [SECP](https://eservices.secp.gov.pk/eServices/?q=/e-services-2/) and [FBR](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/categ/register-income-tax/51147/30846/61149) are distinct processes. Free software does not automatically establish a company or a specific registration requirement.
5. Review current data-protection legislation/status, PECA, electronic transactions, consumer and tax rules as applicable. Do not assume a particular personal-data bill has or has not commenced. For starting points see [PECA](https://www.pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator6a061efe0ed5bd153fa8b79b8eb4cba7.pdf) and [Electronic Transactions Ordinance listing](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/english/sHyuRiF.php). Document relevant professional advice and revisit on product changes.
