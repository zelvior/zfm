Packaging accepts externally built binaries and does not verify toolchain versions, so review additional applicable notices when using a different toolchain or payload.

The project describes Windows setup as unsigned; released artifacts were not independently signature-verified in this audit. Publisher/version metadata is self-declared and does not establish identity. SHA256SUMS help detect corruption only when the checksum is obtained from a trusted channel; unsigned checksums are not authenticity proof. Obtain a legitimate code-signing certificate only after identity/authority checks, protect signing keys, use timestamping and verify signatures on the final artifacts. Do not claim code-signing or trust until verified. Linux repository/package signing similarly requires real keys, secure distribution and appropriate verification. Do not publish until owners approve unresolved rights and runtime obligations.
