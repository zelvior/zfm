# International distribution checklist

**NEEDS REVIEW.** Distribution to Windows/Linux users in other territories does not itself establish compliance with each country's law. Identify actual distribution territories, operator, users, collection and commercial model. With counsel assess applicable privacy/data transfer, consumer and digital product, accessibility, sanctions/export controls, copyright and trademark requirements and local language rules. Avoid asserting GDPR, UK GDPR, US state or other regime compliance without applicability and implementation analysis. Apache rights are worldwide as stated in the license but do not waive mandatory local law or third-party rights. Use [trademark search guidance](TRADEMARKS.md); an international filing does not cover every territory. Record decisions and revisit when activities change.
